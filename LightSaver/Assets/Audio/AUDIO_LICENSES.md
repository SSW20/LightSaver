# LightSaver audio sources

The source recordings below are redistributed under the listed licenses. The
source pages and processing are recorded here so the project can trace every
sound and preserve required attribution.

| LightSaver file | Source | Author | License / processing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PlayerFootstep.wav` | [Steps in wood floor](https://opengameart.org/content/steps-in-wood-floor) | mikeask | CC0; one clean shoe step was trimmed and normalized. |
| `PlayerHeartbeat.wav` | [Real heartbeat sound](https://freesound.org/people/under_the_hood/sounds/455089/) | under_the_hood | CC0; a steady section of a real human heartbeat recording was trimmed, converted to mono PCM, faded at the loop boundary, and normalized. |
| `MonsterFootstep.wav` | [Steps in wood floor](https://opengameart.org/content/steps-in-wood-floor) | mikeask | CC0; derived from the player step, slightly lengthened and layered with a subtle original low thud to match the Tree Ent walk cycle. |
| `PlayerBreath1.wav`, `PlayerBreath2.wav`, `PlayerBreath3.wav` | [Breathing Tired](https://opengameart.org/content/breathing-tired) | mikeask | CC0; one recording was normalized and made into three subtle speed/length variations for randomized playback. |
| `PlayerDamage.wav` | [Screaming (`Scream 3.wav`)](https://opengameart.org/content/screaming) | Dan Knoflicek / Macro | CC-BY 3.0; normalized and faded. Required credit: **SFX by Dan Knoflicek**. |
| `GeneratorComplete.wav` | [Pleasing Bell Sound Effect](https://opengameart.org/content/pleasing-bell-sound-effect) | Spring Spring / Julie Damsgaard | CC0; normalized and converted to mono for 3D audio. |
| `MonsterAttack.wav` | [Osare Minotaur Sounds (`mino1.wav`)](https://opengameart.org/content/osare-minotaur-sounds) | Brandon Morris / HaelDB | CC0 option selected; strongest attack call was trimmed, pitch-lowered, layered with an original low growl, and converted to mono. |
| `BackgroundMusic.wav` | [Unsolved Investigation](https://opengameart.org/content/unsolved-investigation) | isaiah658 | CC0; normalized and kept stereo. The source is authored as a seamless loop. |
| `FlashlightOff.wav` | [87 Clickety Clips (`click30.wav`)](https://opengameart.org/content/87-clickety-clips) | OwlishMedia | CC0; normalized and converted to the project's PCM WAV format. Both flashlight ON and OFF share this one sound. |
| `UIClick.wav` | [UI Sound Effects (`click_3.wav`)](https://opengameart.org/content/ui-sound-effects-button-clicks-user-feedback-notifications) | Robin Lamb | CC0; normalized and converted to the project's PCM WAV format. |
